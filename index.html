Craps

Shooter
PASS 1:1 · DON'T PASS 1:1 (12 pushes) · ODDS pay true odds

Set a chip value, bet on the Pass Line, then ROLL.

How it works

Come-out roll: 7 or 11 wins the Pass Line, 2, 3 or 12 loses it (Don't Pass wins on 2 and 3, pushes on 12). Any other number becomes the point. Then roll the point again before a 7 to win Pass. Odds can be added once a point is set (3-4-5× rule: up to 3× on 4/10, 4× on 5/9, 5× on 6/8): Pass odds pay 2:1 on 4/10, 3:2 on 5/9, 6:5 on 6/8. One-roll bets (Field, Any 7, Any Craps, Eleven) resolve every roll.
